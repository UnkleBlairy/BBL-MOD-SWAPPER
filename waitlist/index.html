What it is

A custom 3D-printed bottom panel for your FM-1, prefitted with the hardware mod: 2 USB-Cs and an audio in jack.

  • Swap between ported FM-1 firmwares from a menu.
  • SD card: heaps of samples, save states for every ported firmware.
  • Audio in for supported firmware (BBL Jam Box now): play through it, record live loops, sample to the card.
  • Runs off the FM-1's own battery and switches on and off with it. No power bank, no extra leads.
